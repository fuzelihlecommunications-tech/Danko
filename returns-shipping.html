Policy

Returns & Shipping

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short: we do not give refunds. We do not exchange designs or colours. The only exchange we make is for a different size. Shipping is a flat R60 per order.

Returns and refunds

All sales are final. Danko Is Thank You does not offer refunds on change of mind, wrong size or a design or colour you no longer want. Please check the product photos and choose your size carefully before you order.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under South African consumer law, including your rights if an item is faulty or not as described.

Exchanges

  • We do not exchange designs.
  • We do not exchange colours.
  • We only exchange sizes.

Sizes run from XXS to XXL and every size is in stock. To ask for a size exchange, email DankoBFN@gmail.com or call +27 72 739 2689 with your order details and the size you need. The item must be unworn, unwashed and in its original condition.

Faulty or damaged items

If your item arrives damaged or faulty, contact us as soon as you notice it, with photos, and we will put it right in line with your legal rights.

Shipping

  • Shipping fee: flat R60 per order.
  • Processing time: orders are processed within 2–3 business days.
  • Delivery time: depends on the courier and your area. We will share delivery details when your order ships.
  • Order tracking: we do not offer order tracking at this time.

Please make sure your delivery address and phone number are correct. We are not responsible for orders delayed or lost because of an incorrect address.

Questions

Email DankoBFN@gmail.com or call +27 72 739 2689.
