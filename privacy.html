Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Danko Is Thank You (Pty) Ltd ("Danko", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who we are

Danko Is Thank You is a streetwear label based in Bloemfontein, South Africa. For any privacy question, email DankoBFN@gmail.com or call +27 72 739 2689.

Information we collect

  • Order information: your name, delivery address, phone number and email, and the items you order.
  • Account information: your name and email if you create an account.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form, email or phone, and your email if you join our mailing list.
  • Technical information: basic data such as browser type and pages visited, to keep the site working.

Payments are processed by Yoco on its own secure page. We never see or store your card details. Yoco handles your payment information under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To reply to your questions and arrange size exchanges.
  • To run your account.
  • To send news about drops and collections, only if you joined our list. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the site secure and improve it.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, such as couriers, payment providers and email or website services, and only as much as they need to do their job. We may also share information where the law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

This site stores your bag, wishlist and sign-in details in your browser so they are still there when you return. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may object to marketing at any time. Email DankoBFN@gmail.com to do this. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with a new date.
